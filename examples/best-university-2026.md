# Best University in 2026: Oxford on THE, MIT on QS

## Summary
There is no single "best" university, because each ranking weights different things. The Times Higher Education (THE) 2026 rankings put the University of Oxford first for the tenth straight year [1]. I could not open the QS 2026 table, but search results list MIT as the top-ranked university there, and I have not verified that directly.

## Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings 2026
- The 2026 edition ranked 2,191 institutions from 115 countries and territories [1].
- Oxford retained the number one spot for the tenth consecutive year, driven by a strong research environment score [1].
- Princeton rose to joint third, its best-ever finish, and was the only US university to reach a new peak that year [1].
- China had five universities in the top 40, up from three the year before. Hong Kong held a record six spots in the top 200 [1].

## QS World University Rankings 2026 (not verified)
- Search results describe MIT as the best university in the world in the QS 2026 edition, with over 1,500 universities included [2]. The QS site blocked my access, so I could not confirm this on the page itself.
- QS weights academic reputation (40%), employer reputation (15%), faculty/student ratio (15%), citations per faculty (20%), and internationalization (10%) [3]. This explains why its top spots differ from THE's, which leans more on research.

## Other lists
- TIME's 2026 ranking also places Oxford and Cambridge near the top (ranked no. 1 and no. 7) [4]. It emphasizes students' later success, such as patents and leadership roles.

## Which to use
- For the broadest research-based comparison, use THE (Oxford first).
- For reputation and employer perception, QS is more useful (MIT first, per search results).
- For a specific subject, check the subject-level rankings, since the top school often differs by field.
